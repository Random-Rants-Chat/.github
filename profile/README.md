## 🚀 What is Random Rants +?

This isn't your average corporate chat app. It started as a way for middle schoolers to escape the school filter and has evolved into a feature-packed hangout hub.

### 🎭 Things You Can Do
* **Video & Voice Chat**: Scream loud and make goofy faces. 
* **Screen Sharing**: Only one share per room—prepare for battles over who controls the screen.
* **Soundboard**: Meme sounds and goofy noises synced for everyone. *Warning: Watch your volume!*
* **File Uploads**: Memes, cursed content, whatever—drop it in.
* **Interactive Media**: A virtual TV where anyone can plug in content, including:
    * **Shared Painting Canvas** 🖌️
    * **Scratch Cloud Mini-games** (via modified TurboWarp) 🐱
    * **Embedded Sites** 🌐
* **Chat Commands**: Use `;help` to see what power you have.

### 🛡️ Ownership & Moderation
* **No Filters**: We don't censor any user content. It’s simple as that.
* **Room Control**: The room creator (Owner) is the ultimate boss. They can grant "Ownership" to others. Owners can ban, block, or clear chat.
* **Unlisted Rooms**: Every room is private by default. Share the URL, Quick Join code, or invite by username.

### 👤 Profiles
Everyone who signs up gets a profile. It's unlisted but visible when you join a room:
* **Username**: Locked once you sign up—it's how we identify you.
* **Display Names**: Nicknames with almost any character/emoji. Change it any time.
* **User Colors & Fonts**: Make your name pop in the chat.
* **Profile Picture**: Identify yourself with whatever image you want.

## 🛠️ Technical Note
* **Privacy**: Video/Audio streams go straight through **WebRTC**. No "sus" servers are watching your face or screen.
* **Environment**: Developed largely on a Chromebook using devboxes. ChatGPT and Google Gemini helped polish the code, but the heart of the project is hand-written.
* **Teen Humor**: This place was made by middle schoolers, for middle schoolers. Humor varies; if something feels too "sus" or not for you, you can always hop out of the room.
